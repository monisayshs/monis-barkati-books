Privacy Policy — Monis Barkati Books

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Monis Barkati Books is an independent publisher of large-print puzzle books, sold through Amazon KDP. This page explains how our Pinterest developer app ("Monis Barkati Books Pin Publisher") handles information.

What our app does

Our app connects to our own Pinterest business account (Monis Barkati Books) through Pinterest's official API. We use it only to create and schedule Pins on our own boards. Those Pins link to our own book pages on Amazon.

What information the app accesses

The app accesses only our own Pinterest account content — our boards and our Pins — as needed to publish and verify our posts. The app is used solely by the account owner. It has no other users.

What we do not do

We do not collect, store, sell, rent, or share personal information about Pinterest users or any other third parties. We do not track visitors, run advertising profiles, or pass data to data brokers. No information from Pinterest is kept on any external server beyond what is needed to operate the app for our own account.

Cookies and tracking

This website and our app do not set tracking cookies and do not use analytics services.

Your choices

Because our app works only on our own account and does not gather data about anyone else, there is no personal data of yours for us to access, correct, or delete. If you have any question about this policy, write to us and we will reply personally.
